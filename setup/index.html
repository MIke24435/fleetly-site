Setup guide

Duplicate the Fleetly Notion Template, add your license key, then hand the rest to the Fleetly bot.

  1. 1. Duplicate the Fleetly Notion Template Sign in to Notion (create a free account at notion.so if you need one). Open Open the Fleetly Notion Template and choose Duplicate (top right) to copy it into your workspace.
  2. 2. Paste your license key In the duplicated template, open the Settings database and paste your key into the License key field. The key arrives in your purchase receipt email and looks like FL-XXXXX-XXXXX.
  3. 3. Connect Notion to the Fleetly bot In the Fleetly bot, connect Notion, choose your duplicated Fleetly Notion Template page, and set each permission to Always available, not Ask. An Ask setting can block database queries.
  4. 4. Add the Fleetly bot to Grok Bot Add the Fleetly bot to your Grok account. The Fleetly bot runs your fleet and reports to your Chief of Staff. Add Fleetly to Grok Bot.
  5. 5. Fill in the onboarding form The form builds a FLEETLY SETUP block labeled to match your Settings properties: Owner name, Email, License key, Grok Bot Email Inbox, Health check (day, time, and timezone), Coordinator name, and Field AIs. Fleetly accepts mail from you and Fleetly updates. Add other addresses later in Settings. Your AIs are added when you connect them. Open the onboarding form
  6. 6. Paste the block into the Fleetly bot Paste the generated block into the Fleetly bot’s chat (the bot from Step 4), not into a Chief of Staff bot, and say connect Fleetly: (alias complete my setup). The Fleetly bot fills in your Settings and AI Fleet databases from that block.
  7. 7. Smoke test In the Fleetly bot’s chat, say my tasks:. If it lists your tasks, Notion is wired up. Your AIs are checked later, when you connect each one. Say help: anytime to see what your AIs can do.

Skills

Skills are plain-text rows in the Fleetly Notion Template’s Skills database. They are already in the copy you duplicated. The Fleetly bot reads those rows. There is no Fleet Skills database, no personalised Fleet Onboarding page, and no step that fetches skills from an API or a URL.

AI lineup

The Fleetly Notion Template’s lineup is:

  • Grok Bot
  • Muse
  • Claude
  • ChatGPT
  • Chief of Staff (your primary bot)
  • Fleetly bot
