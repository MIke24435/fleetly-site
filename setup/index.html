Setup guide

Duplicate the template, add your license key, then hand the rest to Fleetly.

  1. 1. Duplicate Fleetly Notion Template Create a free Notion account at notion.so if you need one. Open Fleetly Notion Template and click Duplicate in the top-right.
  2. 2. Paste your license key In the duplicated template, open the Settings database and paste your key into the License key field. The key arrives in your purchase receipt email and looks like FL-XXXXX-XXXXX.
  3. 3. Set Notion capabilities to Always available Before the smoke test, set every Notion capability for the AI to Always available, not Ask.
  4. 4. Add Fleetly on Grok Add Fleetly to your Grok account. Fleetly is the bot that runs your fleet and reports to your Chief of Staff. Open the Grok Bot template.
  5. 5. Fill in the onboarding form The form builds a FLEETLY SETUP block labeled to match your Settings properties: Owner name, Email, Address, Phone, License key, Updates email, Health check, Allowed senders, Chief of Staff, Timezone, and Field AIs. Allowed senders starts with updates@fleetlybots.com, the address Fleetly uses for skill update emails. Add any other addresses you want. Leave that one in the list unless you mean to block those emails. Open the onboarding form
  6. 6. Paste the block into Fleetly Paste the generated block into Fleetly’s chat and say complete my setup. Fleetly fills in your Settings from that block.
  7. 7. Smoke test Say my tasks: to Fleetly. If it lists your tasks, setup is done. Say help: anytime to see what your AIs can do.

Skills

Skills are plain-text rows in the template’s Skills database. They are already in the template you duplicated. Fleetly reads those rows. There is no Fleet Skills database, no personalised Fleet Onboarding page, and no step that fetches skills from an API or a URL.

AI lineup

The template’s lineup is:

  • Grok
  • Claude
  • ChatGPT
  • Muse
  • Chief of Staff (your primary bot)
  • Fleetly
