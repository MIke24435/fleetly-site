Your onboarding checklist

0 of 2 steps complete

  1. 1. Duplicate the Template Copy the Fleetly Template into your Notion workspace. Open the template and click Duplicate in the top-right.
  2. 2. Connect Your AI Connect your Chief AI to Notion (and any other connectors it needs). Each AI has its own settings — look for Integrations or Connectors.
  3. 3. AgentMail Setup Create inboxes and connect each AI. You'll need these addresses for the next step. Coming soon
  4. 4. Basic Setup License key, contact info, your Chief, and AI email addresses. Generates your setup block.
  5. 5. AI Fleet Roster Name your AIs and assign their roles. Coming soon
  6. 6. Notification Preferences How your Chief reaches you, and when to stay quiet. Coming soon
  7. 7. Health Check Config When your Chief runs its weekly health check, and which email addresses may contact it. Part of the Basic Setup form below.
