Set up Fleetly

Work through the checklist below. The last step builds a setup block that you paste into the Fleetly bot’s chat.

Your onboarding checklist

Do these in order. Your progress is saved automatically.

0 of 6 steps complete

  1. Step 0: Have a Fleetly supervisor bot Your Fleetly supervisor is the bot you give most of your requests to, usually your Chief of Staff. It passes Fleetly work to the Fleetly bot. If you don't have one, create one from the Chief of Staff template first, then enter its name in Basic Setup below — it's required.
  2. Step 1: Duplicate the Fleetly Notion Template Sign in to Notion (create a free account at notion.so if you need one). Click Open the Fleetly Notion Template, then choose Duplicate (top right) to copy it into your workspace. Come back here when you’re done.
  3. Step 3: Connect Notion to the Fleetly bot In the Fleetly bot, connect the Notion plugin to Grok Bot, choose your duplicated Fleetly Notion Template page, and set each permission to Always available, not Ask. See the Setup Guide.
  4. Step 4: Add the Fleetly bot to Grok Bot Add the Fleetly bot to your Grok account. The Fleetly bot runs your fleet and reports to your Fleetly supervisor.
  5. Step 5: Basic Setup Your details for the Settings database. Generates the setup block you paste into Fleetly.
  6. Step 6: Paste the setup block Copy your setup block below and paste it into the Fleetly bot’s chat (the bot from Step 4), not into your Fleetly supervisor bot, then say connect Fleetly: (alias: complete my setup).

Progress is saved on this device only.

Step 5: Basic Setup

License key

Paste this key into the Fleetly Notion Template’s Settings database, in the License key field. (Setup Guide step 2.)

It is in your purchase receipt email and looks like FL- followed by two groups of 5 letters or numbers.

That doesn’t look like a Fleetly key. It should look like FL-XXXXX-XXXXX.
