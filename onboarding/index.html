  1. Step 0: Connect Notion Create a free Notion account at notion.so if you don't have one. You'll duplicate the Fleetly Notion Template into it next. Before the smoke test, connect Notion to the Fleetly bot as shown in the Setup Guide.
  2. 1. Duplicate the Fleetly Notion Template Sign in to Notion, click Open the Fleetly Notion Template, then choose Duplicate (top right) to copy it into your workspace. Come back here when you’re done.
  3. 2. Add the Fleetly bot to Grok Bot Add the Fleetly bot to your Grok account. The Fleetly bot runs your fleet and reports to your Chief of Staff.
  4. 3. Basic Setup Your details for the Settings database. Generates the setup block you paste into Fleetly.

Progress is saved on this device only.

License key
