Set up Fleetly

Installing Fleetly has 2 parts. Part 1 sets up your Fleetly bot (Grok Bot), which reports to your Fleetly supervisor — that is what this form is for. Part 2 connects your AI fleet, and comes after Part 1.

Installing another AI? Go right to Part 2.

Part 1 - Connecting your Grok Bot

Before you start: have a Notion and an AgentMail account (about 10 minutes). Open the Setup Checklist.

Do these in order; step numbers match the Setup Guide. Your progress is saved automatically.

0 of 16 steps complete

  1. Step 0: Have a Fleetly supervisor bot · Where: Grok Bot app Your Fleetly supervisor is the bot you give most of your requests to, usually your Chief of Staff. It passes work to the Fleetly bot. If you don't have one, open the Chief of Staff template and click Add to Grok Bot (If Chrome asks “Open Grok Bot?”, click Open.) — the Add dialog has no name field, so don't look for one. Then enter the name you want Fleetly to use for it in Fleetly supervisor name in Basic Setup below — it's required.
  2. Step 1: Duplicate the Fleetly Notion Template · Where: Notion First sign in to Notion — the Duplicate icon only appears once you are signed in. Click Open the Fleetly Notion Template, then click the Duplicate icon at the top right. In the window that opens, pick the workspace you'll use for Fleetly.
  3. Step 2: Connect Notion plugin · Where: Grok Bot app Connect the Notion plugin in Grok Bot. Note: the sign-in screens say “Cursor” — that is the Grok Bot app’s own sign-in; sign in to continue. It sets up an Account linked to the workspace holding your template copy. Sign in to that workspace, choose the template page, and click Allow access. In Grok Bot, set the Notion plugin permission to Always allow, not Auto-review. Test it: in Grok Bot, type Open my Fleetly Notion Template Settings and tell me the last 5 characters of the License key. You should see 5 characters that match the end of the License key in your Settings. (The my tasks: test comes in Step 7, after the Fleetly bot is added.) See the Setup Guide.
  4. Step 3: Connect the AgentMail plugin · Where: Grok Bot app Connect the AgentMail plugin in Grok Bot — no API key is needed. If Grok Bot asks you to sign in, use the same email as your AgentMail account. (If no sign-in appears, you are already signed in — carry on.) Test it: in Grok Bot, type List my AgentMail inboxes. This lets the Fleetly bot see email activity. You create the inboxes yourself in the AgentMail console at Step 9. See the Setup Guide.
  5. Step 4: Add the Fleetly bot · Where: Grok Bot app Add the Fleetly bot to your Grok Bot account. (If Chrome asks “Open Grok Bot?”, click Open.) The Fleetly bot runs your fleet and reports to your Fleetly supervisor.
  6. Step 5: Basic Setup · Where: This form Your details for the Settings database. Generates the setup block you paste into the Fleetly bot’s chat.

          Step 9: Create your AIs’ email inboxes · Where: AgentMail console
          The Fleetly bot cannot create inboxes yet, so create them yourself at console.agentmail.to: one inbox per AI, named [username]-chatgpt, [username]-muse and [username]-claude (use your AI Fleet username from Basic Setup, lowercase, no spaces). Part 2 - Connecting your AI Fleet

    
Connect one AI at a time, in this order: ChatGPT first, then Muse, then Claude. The first AI takes about 15 minutes; each later AI takes about 10 minutes. The free Claude plan gives you a set amount of use every 5 hours, and connecting it takes quite a bit of that. If Claude says you’ve reached your limit, nothing is broken — come back after the time it shows and type continue Claude in the Fleetly bot’s chat. Full steps are in Part 2 of the Setup Checklist. Not sure where a command goes? See Where do I type this?
